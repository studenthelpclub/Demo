Solved assignment reference material FAQs

Can I submit these typed assignments directly to IGNOU?

No. IGNOU guidelines state that assignments must be handwritten only - typed assignments are not accepted. Our material is reference/study material only: read it, understand it, then write your own assignment in your own handwriting and submit that.

Are these answers guaranteed to get high marks?

No. Marks depend entirely on what you write in your own assignment and how IGNOU evaluates it. No marks or grades are guaranteed, and nobody can promise an evaluation outcome.

Are you affiliated with IGNOU?

No. TaskNest AI is an independent private study-material service and is not affiliated with IGNOU. Question papers are published free on IGNOU's official website.

Which subjects are available?

Availability changes by session. Send your programme and subject codes on WhatsApp and we will confirm which reference answers are available and the charge before you pay.

Where do I get the assignment question papers?

From IGNOU's official assignment portal (webservices.ignou.ac.in/assignments) - they are published there free of cost.

How and where do I submit my assignment?

You write your assignment by hand and submit it at your Study Centre (LSC) as per your Regional Centre's official guidelines and last date. We do not submit assignments on anyone's behalf.

Is copying the reference answer as-is okay?

No. Copying reference material word-for-word defeats the purpose and can hurt your evaluation. Use it to understand the answer, then write in your own words and handwriting.

Do you provide assignments for other universities?

No. Reference material is offered only for IGNOU subjects, so the focus and quality stay specific to IGNOU students.
